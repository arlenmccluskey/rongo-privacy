Rongo privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

Rongo is an app for listening to field recordings of wild places in New Zealand. This page explains what the app does with your information. In short: listening needs no account, and Rongo does not track you.

If you just listen

You don't need an account. Rongo doesn't collect your name, email, location or contact details to play places. Your visits and notes are kept on your phone. The app loads places, photos and audio from our servers, which see your device's internet address for the moment of the request, as any website does. We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking.

If you sign in as a creator

A small number of people can record and publish places. If that's you, you sign in with Sign in with Apple. We store the email address Apple gives us (which may be Apple's private relay address) so we know who published what. We don't use it for marketing and we don't share it.

What creators upload

  • Recordings and photos. The recording's listening copy and the photos you choose are uploaded and shown to everyone. Photos are saved without their location data, and the original photo is never uploaded.
  • Location. The place's true location is stored privately and only the creator can read it. Listeners are shown only what the creator chose: an exact pin, an approximate circle (about 1 km, centred away from the true spot) or a region.
  • Names, notes and birds typed into a place are shown with it.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone, to record.
  • Location, while the app is open, to help pin where you recorded.
  • Photos and camera, to add photos of the place.
  • Speech recognition, to turn the spoken intro into a suggested name. This happens on your phone where your device supports it.

You can change any of these in the Settings app.

Who stores the data

Account details and place information are stored with Supabase, and audio and photos with Cloudflare. They process data for us and don't use it for their own purposes.

Deleting your data

Creators can delete their drafts and published places in the app. To delete your account and everything linked to it, email us at the address below and we will do it.

Children

Rongo isn't aimed at children and doesn't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this changes (for example when a journal or intentions book comes to the app), we'll update this page and the date above.
